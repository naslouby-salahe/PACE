\section{Complete Experimental Comparison Inventory}
\label{app:inventory}
\setcounter{table}{0}
All entries below use the 58-target primary cohort and seeds 6--8. PACE is listed with all 12 executed internal study arms and both recorded baselines. Differences subtract the listed rule's target-macro TPR from PACE's before rounding. Intervals use the same corpus-stratified paired bootstrap as the primary comparison; ablation contrasts are exploratory. The PACE self-comparison has a zero interval by definition. All rates and differences are proportions. Family removals refer to bank experts, not removal of corresponding anchor members.

PACE & Separate margin-evidence peer union, independently calibrated six-detector mean anchor, and heterogeneous six-expert bank; $r=a=0.5$.\\
Mean local & Primary comparator: identical peer union plus the mean evidence of seven local detectors, including the autoencoder, thresholded at $B_L$.\\
No feature & Remove the bank IF, histogram, and Gaussian experts; retain anchor, rarity and response experts, and recalibrate the bank.\\
No response & Remove the peer-response IF, retaining the anchor and feature/rarity bank; recalibrate the bank.\\
No rarity & Remove ECOD-sum and pair-surprisal experts; retain anchor, feature and response experts, and recalibrate the bank.\\
Bank only & Remove the anchor and calibrate the six-expert bank at the entire local allocation $B_L$; the peer branch is unchanged.\\
Single union & Jointly calibrate anchor and bank within $B_L$: initial anchor share $a$, with $(1-a)/6$ for each bank expert.\\
Raw response & Fit the response IF on raw peer margins instead of target-relative evidence; retain the other components and tiers.\\
Reserve 0.25 & Set $r=0.25$ for the tiered PACE rule, including the implemented anchor-resolution condition; peer allocation is $0.75\alpha$.\\
Peer mean & Mean-fuse peer margin evidence into one calibrated expert at $B_P$; retain the complete PACE anchor and bank.\\
Peer max & Maximum-fuse peer margin evidence into one calibrated expert at $B_P$; retain the complete PACE anchor and bank.\\
Peers only & Remove the entire local branch and calibrate the separate-peer union at the full $\alpha$; this is not the extracted $B_P$ peer branch.\\
Local only & Remove peers and threshold the seven-detector mean local ensemble at the full $\alpha$; this is not the extracted PACE local branch.\\
Recorded max & Maximum of probability-tail peer evidence, thresholded once at $B_P$, plus the seven-detector mean local branch at $B_L$.\\
Recorded Bonf. & Separate margin-evidence peers at fixed equal shares of $B_P$, multiplier one, plus the seven-detector mean local branch at $B_L$.\\
