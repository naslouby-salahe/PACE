## Remaining boundaries

These are verified limits, not unfinished manuscript edits:

1. **External validity:** the benchmark informed development. Untouched targets/captures and comparable external baselines remain necessary for broader superiority claims.
2. **Operating points:** sparse frozen sweeps and outcome-dependent support cannot establish general equal-FPR superiority or an onboarding threshold-selection procedure.
3. **Operational behavior:** extreme target FPR, trustworthy onboarding/peers, privacy, adversarial robustness, causal streaming and resource/incident costs remain unvalidated. The manuscript makes no deployment guarantee.
4. **Dataset provenance:** some retrieval/version and redistribution records are unavailable. Cache/campaign hashes do not certify original raw-archive byte identity. A prospective pre-outcome design freeze cannot be reconstructed from available history.
5. **Public reproducibility:** public implementation/configurations/result tables are verified at `41b3a18c10422fc41d1785c35881ccd6323941fa`. Detailed local campaign JSONs and cache manifests are needed by reporting verifiers; their public distribution is not claimed.
6. **Journal adaptation:** no journal is selected. Official Internet of Things and Computer Networks formatting guides returned 403, including alternate Elsevier links. IEEE supplementary/disclosure requirements were subsequently verified through the official Author Center and IEEE Open. This prevents claiming a fully checked submission package for those venues. Computers & Security's official ML moratorium excludes it as a current recommendation.
7. **Submission declarations:** user confirms approval, no funding and no competing interests and requests omission from the master. That request is implemented. Journals requiring declarations/roles need a submission-specific adaptation; missing individual roles and coauthor ORCIDs were not invented. Accurate AI-assistance wording is prepared in the report.

Four independent reviewer roles were completed, with statistical and security corrections rechecked. No remaining manuscript-only correction was identified within those reviews.
